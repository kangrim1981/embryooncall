Consent & Non-Circumvention Agreement

This agreement is shown at the request & deposit step. Please read it in full and sign below before submitting — your request does not go through without a signature. Travel is reimbursed at exact cost.

Confidentiality & consent

  1. Signature required before placement. The lab must sign this agreement before any placement begins, before the embryologist’s contact details are released, and before the first day of coverage.
  2. Confidential information. Embryologist details and contact information shared for a placement are confidential and may not be shared or used outside the placement.
  3. Contact through EmbryoOnCall only. Labs may not contact the embryologist directly. All communication, scheduling, extensions, and future bookings must go through EmbryoOnCall.
  4. Consent to proceed. By signing, the lab consents to EmbryoOnCall matching staff to this request, sharing the request details with the clinicians proposed for the placement, and invoicing as described in the lab agreement & terms — 50% deposit up front, final invoice for the remaining balance, and travel at exact cost.

Non-circumvention

Every booking for an embryologist we introduce goes through EmbryoOnCall — including re-hires. No private contact, no going direct.

  1. 12 months — all bookings go through EmbryoOnCall. For 12 months after EmbryoOnCall introduces an embryologist to a lab, the lab may not hire, re-hire, or privately contact that embryologist directly. All bookings — including re-hires, extensions, and future assignments — go through EmbryoOnCall.
  2. Direct-hire fee if the lab goes direct. If the lab hires, re-hires, or engages the embryologist directly within those 12 months, the lab owes EmbryoOnCall a direct-hire fee of $5,000, or 20% of whatever the lab pays the embryologist directly during those 12 months — whichever is higher.
  3. Loss of access. A lab that circumvents EmbryoOnCall loses access to EmbryoOnCall placements — no further placements or coverage.
  4. The embryologist agrees to the same. Embryologists placed by EmbryoOnCall agree to the same rule on their side: they may not accept direct work from a lab EmbryoOnCall introduced them to. All such work is booked through EmbryoOnCall.
