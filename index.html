Lab agreement & terms

By requesting coverage, the lab agrees to the terms below, including the travel & payment terms and the non-circumvention clause. The confidentiality agreement must be signed and the 50% deposit paid before staff are placed or contact details are released.

  • 1

    Embryologist responsibility

    If a mistake occurs during a procedure, responsibility lies with the embryologist who performed the work. EmbryoOnCall coordinates the placement only and is not liable for clinical errors made by the placed embryologist.

  • 2

    No refunds

    All placements are final. Fees paid for confirmed coverage are non-refundable — no refunds will be issued.

  • 3

    Contact through EmbryoOnCall only

    Labs may not contact the embryologist directly. All communication, scheduling, extensions, and future bookings must go through EmbryoOnCall.

  • 4

    Confidentiality agreement — signature required

    The lab must sign EmbryoOnCall's confidential agreement before any placement begins. Embryologist details, rates, and contact information shared for a placement are confidential and may not be shared or used outside the placement.

  • Signature required — the lab must sign the confidential form before the embryologist's contact details are released and before the first day of coverage.

Travel & payment

How the deposit, invoicing, and travel work for every placement — no surprises on the invoice.

  • 1

    50% deposit required up front

    A 50% deposit is required up front before placement. The embryologist's contact details are not released and placement does not proceed until the deposit is paid and the confidentiality agreement is signed.

  • 2

    How the lab is invoiced

    The day rate is a flat $1,000 per day. The lab is invoiced the day rate × days, plus commission, plus travel at exact cost as its own line item. Commission is 10% for standard / urgent placements, and drops to 7% for multi-day bookings.

  • 3

    Travel — they book, the lab reimburses at exact cost

    The embryologist books their own flight (economy) and hotel, and the lab reimburses at exact cost. The hotel is capped at the GSA per diem rate for that city, and receipts are required for everything. EmbryoOnCall reimburses the embryologist right away out of the deposit — not after the lab's final payment.

  • 4

    Balance due before the assignment starts

    The remaining balance is due before the assignment starts — before the embryologist's first day of coverage.

  • 5

    Cancellation after travel is booked

    If the lab cancels after travel is booked, the deposit covers non-refundable travel. Consistent with the terms above, there are no refunds on confirmed coverage.

  • 6

    If the embryologist can't front the travel cost

    If an embryologist can't front the travel cost, EmbryoOnCall books the flight and hotel directly from the deposit instead.

  • 7

    How the embryologist is paid

    The embryologist is paid their full fee by Zelle after the lab's payment lands, and EmbryoOnCall keeps the commission. The embryologist keeps the full fee — the commission is not taken out of their pay.

Non-circumvention

Every booking for an embryologist we introduce goes through EmbryoOnCall — including re-hires. No private contact, no going direct.

  • 1

    12 months — all bookings go through EmbryoOnCall

    For 12 months after EmbryoOnCall introduces an embryologist to a lab, the lab may not hire, re-hire, or privately contact that embryologist directly. All bookings — including re-hires, extensions, and future assignments — go through EmbryoOnCall.

  • 2

    Direct-hire fee if the lab goes direct

    If the lab hires, re-hires, or engages the embryologist directly within those 12 months, the lab owes EmbryoOnCall a direct-hire fee of $5,000, or 20% of whatever the lab pays the embryologist directly during those 12 months — whichever is higher.

  • 3

    Loss of roster access

    A lab that circumvents EmbryoOnCall loses access to the EmbryoOnCall roster — no further placements or coverage.

  • 4

    The embryologist agrees to the same

    Embryologists on the EmbryoOnCall roster agree to the same rule on their side: they may not accept direct work from a lab EmbryoOnCall introduced them to. All such work is booked through EmbryoOnCall.
