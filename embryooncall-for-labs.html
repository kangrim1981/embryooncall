Lab agreement & terms

By requesting coverage, the lab agrees to the terms below, including the travel & payment terms. The 50% deposit is paid before staff are placed or contact details are released. The consent & non-circumvention agreement is presented for signature as part of submitting a coverage request.

  • 1

    Embryologist responsibility

    If a mistake occurs during a procedure, responsibility lies with the embryologist who performed the work. EmbryoOnCall coordinates the placement only and is not liable for clinical errors made by the placed embryologist.

  • 2

    No refunds

    All placements are final. Fees paid for confirmed coverage are non-refundable — no refunds will be issued.

  • 3

    Contact through EmbryoOnCall only

    Labs may not contact the embryologist directly. All communication, scheduling, extensions, and future bookings must go through EmbryoOnCall.

Travel & payment

How the deposit, invoicing, and travel work for every placement — no surprises on the invoice.

  • 1

    50% deposit required up front

    A 50% deposit is required up front before placement. The embryologist's contact details are not released and placement does not proceed until the deposit is paid.

  • 2

    How the lab is invoiced

    Invoices are sent in two parts — the 50% deposit up front, then the final invoice for the remaining balance, with travel at exact cost as its own line item.

  • 3

    Travel — the lab is responsible for transportation, meals, hotel & flight

    The lab is responsible for the embryologist's transportation (no rental car), meals, hotel, and flights. The embryologist books their own flight (economy) and hotel within policy, and the lab is billed travel at exact cost. The hotel is capped at the GSA per diem rate for that city, and receipts are required for everything. EmbryoOnCall reimburses the embryologist right away out of the deposit — not after the lab's final payment.

  • 4

    Balance due before the assignment starts

    The remaining balance is due before the assignment starts — before the embryologist's first day of coverage.

  • 5

    Cancellation after travel is booked

    If the lab cancels after travel is booked, the deposit covers non-refundable travel. Consistent with the terms above, there are no refunds on confirmed coverage.

  • 6

    Paying by bank or card

    Bank transfer (ACH) is free. If you pay by card, a 2.9% processing fee is added to the invoice.

Confidentiality & consent

  1. Signature required before placement. The lab must sign this agreement before any placement begins, before the embryologist’s contact details are released, and before the first day of coverage.
  2. Confidential information. Embryologist details and contact information shared for a placement are confidential and may not be shared or used outside the placement.
  3. Contact through EmbryoOnCall only. Labs may not contact the embryologist directly. All communication, scheduling, extensions, and future bookings must go through EmbryoOnCall.
  4. Consent to proceed. By signing, the lab consents to EmbryoOnCall matching staff to this request, sharing the request details with the clinicians proposed for the placement, and invoicing as described in the lab agreement & terms — 50% deposit up front, final invoice for the remaining balance, and travel at exact cost.

Non-circumvention

Every booking for an embryologist we introduce goes through EmbryoOnCall — including re-hires. No private contact, no going direct.

  1. 12 months — all bookings go through EmbryoOnCall. For 12 months after EmbryoOnCall introduces an embryologist to a lab, the lab may not hire, re-hire, or privately contact that embryologist directly. All bookings — including re-hires, extensions, and future assignments — go through EmbryoOnCall.
  2. Direct-hire fee if the lab goes direct. If the lab hires, re-hires, or engages the embryologist directly within those 12 months, the lab owes EmbryoOnCall a direct-hire fee of $5,000, or 20% of whatever the lab pays the embryologist directly during those 12 months — whichever is higher.
  3. Loss of access. A lab that circumvents EmbryoOnCall loses access to EmbryoOnCall placements — no further placements or coverage.
  4. The embryologist agrees to the same. Embryologists placed by EmbryoOnCall agree to the same rule on their side: they may not accept direct work from a lab EmbryoOnCall introduced them to. All such work is booked through EmbryoOnCall.
