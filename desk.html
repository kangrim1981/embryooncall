How the PIN lock works

            

              
PIN storage
Only a salted hash (PBKDF2-SHA256) is kept — the PIN itself is never stored or shown.

              
Enforcement
Your PIN derives the password of the desk's Firebase Auth account (). No sign-in, no data: Firestore rules limit the roster, labs, requests, assignments and invoices to that account.

              
Session
Unlocking signs you in for 30 days on this device. “Lock desk” signs you out immediately.

              
Data
Everything lives in the same Firebase project as the traveler portal (embryooncall-travelers), so portal sign-ups land in this roster on their own.
