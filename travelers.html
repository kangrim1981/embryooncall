Traveler Cancellation & Mid-Assignment Departure Terms

Travel Booking Policy — All Flights and Hotels Must Be Refundable

All flights and hotels for an assignment must be booked as refundable, where a refundable option is available. Non-refundable bookings are not permitted unless EmbryoOnCall approves them in writing in advance. If a traveler books a non-refundable flight or hotel without that approval, the traveler is responsible for any non-refundable cost.

Flight Changes and Delays

A. Flight changes: You must not change your flight schedule on your own. You must contact EmbryoOnCall first and get approval before making any change. Any fare difference, change fee, or additional cost resulting from the change is your responsibility, unless the change is required by the lab or EmbryoOnCall.

B. Travel delays: If your flight is delayed, cancelled, or you expect to arrive late to the lab for any travel reason, you must notify EmbryoOnCall first, before contacting the lab, and as soon as you know about the delay. EmbryoOnCall will then notify the lab.

Traveler Cancellation — After You Accept

Once you accept an assignment, your acceptance is a commitment. You may decline any assignment before you accept, with no penalty.

  1. More than 14 days before Day 1: You may cancel with no fee. You must notify EmbryoOnCall immediately, and only through EmbryoOnCall.
  2. 7 to 14 days before Day 1: You must reimburse any non-refundable travel costs already booked for you, and you will lose priority matching for your next assignment.
  3. Within 7 days of Day 1: You must (a) reimburse all non-refundable travel costs already booked for you, (b) pay a $750 cancellation fee, and (c) you will be paused from priority matching for 30 days.
  4. Within 48 hours of Day 1, or no-show: Items (a) and (b) above apply, the fee is $750, and you will be suspended from the roster for 90 days. A second late cancellation or no-show within 12 months will result in removal from the roster.
  5. Emergencies: No fee applies for documented illness, a family emergency, or a flight cancellation beyond your control. You must notify EmbryoOnCall as soon as possible and provide documentation if requested. Non-refundable travel costs are still settled at actual cost.
  6. Collection: Any fee or reimbursement under this section is due within 14 days, or may be deducted from your next assignment pay. Failure to pay will result in removal from the roster. If you do not pay the $750 fee, or any other fee or reimbursement owed under this agreement, within 14 days, EmbryoOnCall may pursue legal action to recover it, and you will be legally responsible for the unpaid amount plus all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys’ fees.

Mid-Assignment Departure — After Day 1 Starts

  1. Notice and handover: If you must leave before your last scheduled day, you must notify EmbryoOnCall immediately by phone, and only through EmbryoOnCall. You must not leave in the middle of a procedure. You must complete a written handover (work completed, embryo status, and any open items) and, unless there is an emergency under Section 9, give at least 48 hours’ notice and stay until a safe handover is possible.
  2. Voluntary early departure: You will be paid only for days actually worked. You must pay for your own return travel, reimburse any non-refundable costs caused by your early departure, pay a $750 early-departure fee, and you will be suspended from the roster for 90 days.
  3. Emergency departure: No fee applies for documented illness, a family emergency, or a flight cancellation beyond your control. You will be paid for days actually worked, and EmbryoOnCall will cover your return travel. Documentation must be provided within 48 hours if requested.
  4. Lab ends the assignment early: If the lab ends your assignment early, you will be paid for days actually worked, and EmbryoOnCall will cover your return travel.
  5. Abandonment: Leaving without notice, or leaving in the middle of the batch without a safe handover, is abandonment. It will result in immediate removal from the roster and a $1,000 abandonment fee, in addition to the $750 early-departure fee in Section 8 — a total of $1,750.

12. Legal Liability

By accepting an assignment, you enter into a binding commitment with EmbryoOnCall. If you cancel, leave early, or fail to show up in breach of this agreement, you will be legally responsible for the losses caused by that breach.

This includes, but is not limited to, the cost of finding and bringing in a replacement traveler, additional travel and lodging costs, and other direct costs actually incurred by EmbryoOnCall or the lab as a result of your cancellation or early departure.

The fees in Sections 3, 4, 8, and 11 are in addition to, and not a substitute for, EmbryoOnCall’s right to pursue any other legal remedies available under this agreement or applicable law.

If EmbryoOnCall must take legal action or use a collection agency to recover any fee, reimbursement, or damages owed under this agreement, you agree to pay all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys’ fees.

This section does not apply to a cancellation or departure covered by Section 5 or 9 — documented emergencies.

13. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Any dispute arising from this agreement will be resolved in the courts of Florida.

Questions, answered

Answers for travelers — how assignments, pay, and travel work. If your question is not here, reach out — we will answer it.

What is per-diem cover?

A lab needs an extra pair of trained hands for a set number of days — someone is sick, on leave, or a busy cycle needs more staff. You step in, do the work for those days, and go home when the cover ends. You pick the dates you are available, and you are never sent somewhere you did not agree to.

How does an assignment work?

A lab tells us the dates and skills it needs. If the dates and skills fit you, we send you the details — the lab, the dates, and what the work covers. You can accept or decline. If you accept, the lab pays a deposit before your contact details are shared, travel is arranged, and you start on day one ready to work.

What roles do you place?

Five roles: Senior Embryologist, Andrologist-CLS, MA (Medical Assistant), Sonographer, and Nurse. Everyone we place has 5+ years of experience.

How do I get paid — and do I keep all of it?

You keep 100% of your pay. The commission is charged to the lab, not taken out of what you earn.

How does travel work?

Travel is paid back at exact cost, and paid promptly. Your expenses are capped: flights are economy class, capped at the market rate plus 25%; hotels are capped at the fair market rate plus 25%; meals are capped at $75 per day; ground transport is capped at $50 per day. All travel is booked through Navan. Keep your receipts — receipts are needed for everything.

How do I set the dates I am available?

You set your own available dates when you join the roster, and you can update them any time. The more dates you list, the more assignments you can match — but you only ever go where you said yes.

Can I do per-diem work while I have another job?

Absolutely. Many per-diem travelers pick up shifts while working a travel assignment or a permanent job — it is a great way to supplement your income. Per-diem cover is flexible by design: you choose the dates you are available and pick up assignments around your own schedule, so you can keep your staff job or another travel job and still earn extra on the days you choose. You only ever go where you said yes — and you keep 100% of your pay.

What happens after I am offered an assignment — can I change my mind?

When you are offered an assignment, you can accept or decline it. If you decline, we offer it to someone else. Nothing is booked until you say yes.

How is per-diem different from a travel contract or a permanent job?

Per-diem means you cover a lab for a few days at a time, when it fits your schedule. A travel contract is a longer stay at one place, usually weeks or months. A permanent job is a full-time staff position at one lab. EmbryoOnCall is per-diem cover only.
